# Sniping the bottom on contracts

This is the first goal. A goal is a lesson. It is not a pattern and it is not an order.

Buy calls on a red candle and puts on a green candle. The bottom of a call is the lowest stock close on a red candle through the confirmed buy. The bottom of a put is the highest stock close on a green candle through the confirmed buy. The first color is not the bottom when a later candle pushes farther.

The number is stock points on the entry candle. That is where the contract is bought. It is not a priced premium.
